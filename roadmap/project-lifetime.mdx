---
title: 'Project lifetime'
description: 'Use lifetime statuses to reflect where a project really is in its lifecycle.'
---

Every roadmap has a **project lifetime status** that reflects its current stage: Active, Maintenance, Sunsetting, or Closed. This status appears in reports and heatmaps so leadership always knows whether a roadmap is under active development or winding down.

Change the lifetime status from the roadmap header or from the roadmaps list. You can filter the portfolio view by lifetime status to focus on only Active projects, for example.

The lifetime status does not affect calculations — a Sunsetting roadmap still shows delivery risk and finance data. It is purely a signal to help readers interpret what they are looking at in reports.

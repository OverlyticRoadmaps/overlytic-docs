---
title: "Project lifetime"
description: "Use lifetime statuses to reflect where a project really is in its lifecycle."
---

Every roadmap has a project lifetime status: Active, Maintenance, Sunsetting, or Closed. This status appears in reports and heatmaps so leadership always knows whether a roadmap is under active development or winding down.

---
title: 'Swimlanes and bars'
description: 'Add and manage swimlanes, bars, and containers in your roadmap timeline.'
---

Swimlanes organise your roadmap into horizontal rows — typically one per team, product area, or workstream. Each swimlane can contain bars (the individual roadmap items) and containers (groups of bars that share a start and end date).

To add a swimlane, click **+ Add swimlane** below the timeline. To add a bar, click inside a swimlane and drag to set its start and end dates, or use the **+ Add item** button. Bars can be resized and moved by dragging directly on the timeline.

Each bar has its own detail panel where you can set the name, owner, description, progress, custom fields, and links to strategy goals.

---
title: "Swimlanes and bars"
description: "Add and manage swimlanes, bars, and containers in your roadmap timeline."
---

Swimlanes organise your roadmap into horizontal rows — typically one per team, product area, or workstream. Each swimlane contains bars (individual roadmap items) and containers (groups of bars that share a start and end date).

---
title: 'Views'
description: 'Switch between Timeline, Table, Horizons, and Grouped views for different perspectives.'
---

Overlytic roadmaps support four views: **Timeline**, **Table**, **Horizons**, and **Grouped**. Each view shows the same underlying data in a format suited to a different audience or task.

Timeline view is the default Gantt-style view with swimlanes, bars, milestones, and a draggable date range. Table view shows all roadmap items as rows with sortable and filterable columns. Horizons groups items by strategic time horizon (Now / Next / Later), and Grouped lets you pivot by any field such as team or status.

Switch views using the toolbar at the top of the roadmap. Your last-used view is remembered per roadmap.

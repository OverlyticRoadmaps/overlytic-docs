---
title: 'Creating a roadmap'
description: 'How to create a new roadmap and configure its basic settings.'
---

To create a roadmap, click **New roadmap** from the dashboard or the roadmaps list in the sidebar. Give it a name, set the owning team, and choose a project lifetime status — Active, Maintenance, Sunsetting, or Closed.

Each roadmap has its own finance settings (approved budget, currency), custom fields, and team membership. You can create as many roadmaps as your plan allows and organise them into folders.

Once created, your roadmap opens in Timeline view ready for you to add swimlanes and bars. You can change the roadmap name, settings, and lifetime status at any time from the roadmap header.

---
title: 'Dependencies'
description: 'Link roadmap items to show blocking relationships and surface dependency risk.'
---

Dependencies let you mark one roadmap item as blocking or blocked by another. Draw a dependency by hovering over a bar until the connection handle appears, then dragging to the target bar.

Overlytic automatically calculates dependency chain risk: if a blocking item is itself at high delivery risk, the risk propagates to all items waiting on it. This surfaces in both the Dependencies heatmap and in generated reports.

You can mark dependencies as internal (within the same roadmap) or external (across roadmaps or to a third-party system). External dependencies show a warning icon when the external item has not been updated recently.

---
title: "Dependencies"
description: "Link roadmap items to show blocking relationships and surface dependency risk."
---

Dependencies let you mark one roadmap item as blocking or blocked by another. Overlytic automatically calculates dependency chain risk and surfaces blocked items in the Dependencies heatmap and in generated reports.

---
title: 'Milestones and launches'
description: 'Mark key dates and track launch readiness with milestones and launch markers.'
---

Milestones are point-in-time markers on the timeline — deadlines, releases, or decision gates. Add a milestone by clicking the milestone icon in the toolbar and placing it on any date in a swimlane.

Launches are special milestones linked to launch readiness scoring. When you mark a date as a Launch, Overlytic tracks the readiness of all roadmap items associated with it and surfaces a readiness score in the Heatmaps view.

Both milestones and launches appear in auto-generated reports, giving leadership a clear view of upcoming key dates without requiring manual input.

---
title: 'What is Overlytic?'
description: 'A one-page overview of what Overlytic does and who it is for.'
---

Overlytic is a roadmap planning tool for project managers and senior leadership. It connects ideas, strategy goals, roadmaps, financial data, and automated reports in one place — so nothing gets lost and leadership always sees what matters.

The platform is designed for two audiences: PMs who need to manage the detail, and leaders who need the signal without the noise. Both see the same data through lenses suited to their role.

You can start with rough, incomplete data and Overlytic will calculate risk, surface dependencies, and generate reports from whatever is there — no spreadsheets required.

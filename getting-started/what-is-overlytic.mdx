---
title: "What is Overlytic?"
description: "A quick overview of what Overlytic does and who it is built for."
---

Overlytic is a roadmap planning tool for project managers and senior leadership. It connects ideas, strategy goals, roadmaps, finance, and automated reports in one place so nothing gets lost and leadership always sees what matters.

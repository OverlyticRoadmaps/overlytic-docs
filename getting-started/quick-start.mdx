---
title: 'Quick start'
description: 'Get your workspace set up and your first roadmap running in under 10 minutes.'
---

This guide walks you through creating your workspace, inviting your first team members, and building your first roadmap. By the end you will have a live roadmap with at least one swimlane and one milestone.

Start by signing up at [app.overlytic.com](https://app.overlytic.com) — your first 30 days are free and no credit card is required. Once inside, create a workspace for your organisation and set your workspace currency and financial year.

From the dashboard, click **New roadmap**, give it a name and a project lifetime status, then add your first swimlane and a couple of bars. Your roadmap is now live and visible to anyone you invite.

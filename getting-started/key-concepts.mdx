---
title: 'Key concepts'
description: 'The core building blocks of Overlytic and how they connect.'
---

Overlytic is built around a connected hierarchy: **Ideas** feed into **Strategy goals**, which drive **Roadmap items**, which are planned in **Scenarios**, tracked by **Time entries**, and surfaced in **Reports**. Understanding this chain helps you get the most out of the platform.

A **Roadmap** is the central object. It belongs to a workspace, has a project lifetime, and contains swimlanes, bars, milestones, and dependencies. Finance, tracking, and heatmap data all attach to roadmap items.

**Heatmaps** are calculated views — not manual inputs. Delivery risk, effort vs progress, and launch readiness are all derived from the data you enter elsewhere, so they stay accurate without any extra work.

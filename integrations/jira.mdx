---
title: "Jira"
description: "Connect Jira to sync issues and projects with your Overlytic roadmap."
---

The Jira integration links Jira issues and epics directly to roadmap bars in Overlytic. Once connected, progress on Jira tickets flows into Overlytic automatically, keeping delivery risk calculations up to date without manual updates.

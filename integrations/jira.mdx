---
title: 'Jira integration'
description: 'Connect Jira to sync issues and projects with your Overlytic roadmap.'
---

The Jira integration allows you to link Jira issues and epics directly to roadmap bars in Overlytic. Once connected, progress on Jira tickets flows into Overlytic automatically, keeping your delivery risk calculations up to date without manual progress updates.

Connect Jira from **Workspace settings → Integrations**. You will need Admin access in both Overlytic and your Jira organisation. Enter your Jira organisation URL and authorise the connection — an access token is stored securely by Overlytic's backend and is never exposed to the browser.

Once connected, open any roadmap bar and select **Link Jira issue** to associate it with an epic or issue. Progress is synced on a scheduled basis and can also be triggered manually.

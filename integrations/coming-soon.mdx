description: 'Integrations planned for future releases: GitHub, Linear, and Slack.'
---

The following integrations are on the roadmap and will be available in upcoming releases. If you have a specific integration need, contact us at [hello@overlytic.com](mailto:hello@overlytic.com) and we will factor it into prioritisation.

**GitHub** — Link pull requests and issues to roadmap bars. Progress and merge status will flow into delivery risk calculations automatically.

**Linear** — Sync Linear issues and cycles with your Overlytic roadmap. Designed for teams using Linear as their primary issue tracker who want portfolio-level visibility in Overlytic.

**Slack** — Receive report delivery notifications and risk alerts in your Slack workspace. Configure which channels receive which signals from Workspace settings.

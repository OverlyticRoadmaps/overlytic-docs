---
title: 'Committing to roadmap'
description: 'Finalise a scenario and push the planned items onto the live roadmap.'
---

When you are happy with a scenario, click **Commit to roadmap** to promote all planned items from that scenario into the live roadmap. Committed items appear immediately in Timeline view with the dates and assignments set during planning.

You can commit one scenario at a time. If you later want to replan, create a new scenario — the previously committed items remain on the roadmap and can be adjusted manually or re-scoped in a new planning session.

A commit is recorded in the roadmap history so you can always see when a planning decision was made and which scenario it came from.

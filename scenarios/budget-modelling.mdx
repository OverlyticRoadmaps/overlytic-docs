---
title: "Budget modelling"
description: "Create and compare multiple budget scenarios before committing to the roadmap."
---

Scenarios let you drag backlog items into PI planning columns and see the cost and capacity impact in real time before anything is committed. Create multiple named scenarios — Baseline, Tight, Stretch — and switch between them to compare outcomes.

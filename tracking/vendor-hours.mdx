---
title: 'Vendor hours'
description: 'Log and manage hours for external vendors against roadmap items.'
---

Vendor hours are logged the same way as internal hours but are attributed to a vendor from the workspace approved vendor list rather than an internal user. Select the vendor from the dropdown when adding a time entry.

Each vendor can have an hourly rate set by an Admin. When hours are logged for that vendor, the cost is calculated automatically and added to the roadmap's vendor spend total.

Vendor hours are visible to workspace Admins and to the vendor if they have been given workspace access. Individual contributors can only see their own entries.

---
title: "Vendor hours"
description: "Log and manage hours for external vendors against roadmap items."
---

Vendor hours are logged the same way as internal hours but are attributed to a vendor from the workspace approved vendor list. Hourly rates set on the vendor record are used to calculate cost automatically.

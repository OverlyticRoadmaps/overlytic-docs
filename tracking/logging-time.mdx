---
title: 'Logging time'
description: 'Track hours against roadmap items using the timer or manual entry.'
---

Time can be logged against any roadmap bar using the built-in timer or by entering hours manually. Open the tracking panel from the roadmap item detail view or from the global Tracking section in the sidebar.

Start the timer when you begin work and stop it when you finish — Overlytic will round to the nearest minute. Alternatively, click **Add entry** to log hours for a past date. Each entry is linked to the roadmap item and to your user account.

Logged hours feed the effort data used in delivery risk calculations and appear in the Effort vs Progress heatmap.

---
title: "Logging time"
description: "Track hours against roadmap items using the timer or manual entry."
---

Time can be logged against any roadmap bar using the built-in timer or by entering hours manually. Logged hours feed the effort data used in delivery risk calculations and appear in the Effort vs Progress heatmap.

---
title: "Delivery risk"
description: "Understand how delivery risk is calculated and what the colours mean."
---

Delivery risk is calculated automatically from two numbers: how far through the timeline a roadmap item is (elapsed percentage) and how complete the item actually is (progress percentage). The gap between these two is the risk score — no manual input required.

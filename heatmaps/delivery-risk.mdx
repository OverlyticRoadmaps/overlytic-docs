---
title: 'Delivery risk'
description: 'Understand how delivery risk is calculated and what the colours mean.'
---

Delivery risk is calculated automatically from two numbers: how far through the timeline a roadmap item is (elapsed percentage) and how complete the item actually is (progress percentage). The gap between these two is the risk score.

Items are coloured **red** when elapsed time significantly exceeds progress, **amber** when the gap is moderate, and **green** when progress is ahead of or matching elapsed time. No manual input is required — the score updates whenever dates or progress change.

The Delivery risk heatmap shows all items across your portfolio so you can identify which roadmaps and teams have the most concentration of at-risk work.

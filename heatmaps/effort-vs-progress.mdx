title: "Effort vs progress"
description: "Find items that are consuming resource but not making proportional progress."
---

The Effort vs Progress heatmap plots every roadmap item on a quadrant: effort logged on one axis, progress made on the other. Items in the high-effort, low-progress quadrant are burning resource without results.

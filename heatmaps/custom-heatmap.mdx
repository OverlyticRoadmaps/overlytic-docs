---
title: 'Custom heatmap'
description: 'Build a heatmap with any axes, metric, and aggregation to answer your specific question.'
---

The custom heatmap lets you choose your own X axis, Y axis, metric, and aggregation to create a coloured matrix tailored to your question. Start from a built-in preset or build from scratch using any combination of roadmap fields.

Available presets include Risk by Team and Quarter, Completion by Epic and Month, and Effort by Status and Team. Each preset can be further customised — swap the axes, change the metric, or adjust the aggregation method (max, average, sum).

The resulting matrix can be exported to PDF and included in reports. Custom heatmaps are saved per workspace so your team can reuse them without rebuilding each time.

---
title: 'Launch readiness'
description: 'Track readiness scores for each planned launch milestone across your portfolio.'
---

Launch readiness is scored per launch milestone based on the progress and dependency status of all roadmap items linked to that launch. A launch is **Ready** when all linked items are on track, **At risk** when some items have moderate delivery risk, and **Not ready** when critical items are blocked or significantly behind.

Scores are calculated automatically and update as progress and dependencies change. No manual RAG updates are needed.

Launch readiness scores appear in this heatmap view and are included in the auto-generated weekly report so leadership always knows whether upcoming launches are on track.

---
title: "PDF export"
description: "Export any report as a landscape PDF with your logo in the header."
---

Any report can be exported as a landscape PDF formatted for presentation. The PDF includes your company logo in the header, the report date, and all sections in print-ready layout — click Export PDF from the top right of any generated report.

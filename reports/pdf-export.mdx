---
title: 'PDF export'
description: 'Export any report as a landscape PDF with your logo in the header.'
---

Any report can be exported as a landscape PDF formatted for presentation. The PDF includes your company logo in the header (configured in workspace settings), the report date, and all sections in print-ready layout.

Click **Export PDF** from the top right of any generated report. The PDF is downloaded immediately — no additional configuration required. Logo upload is available on the Core plan and above.

PDF exports are useful for board packs, investor updates, and any situation where sharing a live link is not appropriate. The PDF is a static snapshot of the report at the time of export.

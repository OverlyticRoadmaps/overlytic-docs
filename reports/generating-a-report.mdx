---
title: 'Generating a report'
description: 'How Overlytic auto-generates boardroom-ready status reports from live data.'
---

Reports in Overlytic are generated automatically from your live roadmap, finance, risk, and dependency data. There is no manual assembly — open the Reports section, select the roadmaps to include, and click **Generate**.

Each report contains an executive summary, portfolio risk overview, finance signals, delivery risk by roadmap, key dependencies, and upcoming milestones. The content is formatted for leadership: enough signal to make decisions, no detail that requires a PM to interpret.

Generated reports are stored in the Reports section and can be regenerated at any time. The latest version always reflects the current state of your data.

---
title: "Scheduling delivery"
description: "Set up automatic report delivery to stakeholder inboxes on a recurring schedule."
---

Reports can be scheduled to send automatically to a list of email addresses on a recurring schedule. The most common use case is a Monday morning delivery to the leadership team — recipients do not need an Overlytic account to receive the email.

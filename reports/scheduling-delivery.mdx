---
title: 'Scheduling delivery'
description: 'Set up automatic report delivery to stakeholder inboxes on a recurring schedule.'
---

Reports can be scheduled to send automatically to a list of email addresses on a recurring schedule — daily, weekly, or on a custom day and time. The most common use case is a Monday morning delivery to the leadership team.

Set up a schedule from **Reports → Scheduled delivery**. Choose which report, the frequency, the send time, and the recipient list. Recipients do not need an Overlytic account to receive the email.

Scheduled reports are generated fresh at send time so they always reflect the latest data. You will receive a notification if a scheduled delivery fails.

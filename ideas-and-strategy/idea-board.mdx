---
title: 'Idea board'
description: 'Capture, tag, and triage ideas before they are ready for the roadmap.'
---

The idea board is a holding area for anything that might become a roadmap item but is not yet planned. Add ideas freely — dates, budgets, and owners are all optional at this stage.

Each idea can be tagged, assigned a category, and linked to one or more strategy goals. Ideas without a goal link are visible in backlog triage so you can decide whether they belong on the roadmap or should be discarded.

Promote an idea to the roadmap when it is ready by clicking **Add to roadmap** from the idea detail panel. The idea's goal links and tags travel with it.

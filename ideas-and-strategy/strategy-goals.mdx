---
title: "Strategy goals"
description: "Define company goals and link them to roadmap items and ideas."
---

Strategy goals represent the outcomes your organisation is trying to achieve. Once defined, you can link any idea or roadmap bar to one or more goals, powering the goal alignment column across all views.

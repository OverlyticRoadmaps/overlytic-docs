---
title: 'Strategy goals'
description: 'Define company goals and link them to roadmap items and ideas.'
---

Strategy goals represent the outcomes your organisation is trying to achieve. Define them in **Ideas & Strategy → Goals** with a name, description, owner, and target date.

Once goals exist, you can link any idea or roadmap bar to one or more goals. This link powers the **Goal alignment** column in the idea board and backlog views, making it easy to see which initiatives are strategically justified and which are not.

Goals also appear in reports, giving leadership a direct line from portfolio activity to company objectives without any manual mapping.

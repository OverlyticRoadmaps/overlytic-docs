---
title: 'Backlog alignment'
description: 'Triage your backlog and identify which items are strategically aligned.'
---

The backlog alignment view shows all ideas and unplanned items alongside their goal alignment status. Items with no goal link are flagged so you can review them before they drift onto the roadmap without justification.

Filter the backlog by alignment status, tag, category, or owner to focus triage sessions. Items can be bulk-updated or archived directly from this view.

Use backlog triage before each planning cycle to keep your idea board healthy — anything without a strategic link should either be linked, parked, or discarded.

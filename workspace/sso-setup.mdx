---
title: 'SSO setup'
description: 'Configure single sign-on and domain provisioning for your workspace.'
---

Overlytic supports SSO via your identity provider, allowing your team to sign in with their existing company credentials. SSO is available on the Core plan and above.

To configure SSO, go to **Workspace settings → Security** and follow the setup wizard for your provider. You will need to provide your identity provider's metadata URL or certificate. Once configured, users on your domain can be automatically provisioned when they first sign in.

Domain provisioning means any new user signing in with your company email domain is automatically added to your workspace with the default role you specify. This removes the need to manually invite every team member.

---
title: 'Workspace settings'
description: 'Configure your workspace name, currency, financial year, and other global options.'
---

Workspace settings are accessible to Admins from the gear icon in the sidebar. This is where you configure options that apply across your entire workspace.

Key settings include your workspace name and logo, default currency, financial year start month, and approved vendor list. The financial year setting affects how budget periods are displayed in Finance and Scenarios views.

Changes to workspace settings take effect immediately for all users. Settings that affect existing data — such as currency — will display a warning before saving.

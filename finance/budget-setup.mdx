---
title: 'Budget setup'
description: 'Set approved budgets and track actual spend per roadmap.'
---

Each roadmap has its own finance panel where you set the approved budget for the period. Budget periods align with your workspace financial year unless you override them at the roadmap level.

Once a budget is set, Overlytic tracks actual spend automatically as time entries with cost rates are logged. The finance view shows approved vs actual at a glance, broken down by in-house and vendor spend.

Budget data flows into the Finance heatmap and into generated reports, flagging any roadmap that is over budget without requiring manual input.

---
title: "Budget setup"
description: "Set approved budgets and track actual spend per roadmap."
---

Each roadmap has its own finance panel where you set the approved budget for the period. Once set, Overlytic tracks actual spend automatically as time entries with cost rates are logged, broken down by in-house and vendor spend.

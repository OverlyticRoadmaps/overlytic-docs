---
title: "Vendor tracking"
description: "Track vendor costs and control what external contributors can see."
---

Vendor costs are logged separately from in-house hours and appear as a distinct line in the Finance view. Each vendor can have an hourly rate set by an Admin so costs are calculated automatically when hours are logged.

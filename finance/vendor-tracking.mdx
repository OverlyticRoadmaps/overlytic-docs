---
title: 'Vendor tracking'
description: 'Track vendor costs and control what external contributors can see.'
---

Vendor costs are logged separately from in-house hours and appear as a distinct line in the Finance view. Vendors can be added to the workspace approved vendor list by an Admin, and each vendor can be given scoped access to specific roadmaps only.

Log vendor hours from the Tracking panel, the same as in-house hours, but select a vendor from the list instead of an internal user. Hourly rates set on the vendor record are used to calculate cost automatically.

Vendor spend is included in the per-roadmap budget vs actual calculation and is flagged separately in Finance signals in reports.
